Federal agencies in the United States have to publish a list of the AI systems they use. That list is called a use case inventory, or a register. The idea behind it is simple: an organisation cannot oversee what it has never written down.

This page is the register the United States federal government published in 2025. Each row is one system an agency has declared, in the agency's own words. You can search it, narrow it, and open any row to see everything the register records about that system.

The register sets aside nine fields for human oversight, filled in only for the entries it flags high-impact, which are the most serious it records. In most of those entries, all nine are empty.

A filled field shows that information was provided, not that the practice is adequate. An empty field is not evidence that the practice is absent.

The pattern describes what reached the file, not the oversight practice itself.
